There's no automation behind it. I found your brand the way a media buyer would: your ads are live, your creative is doing real work, and your traffic is growing. Then I spent an evening inside your funnel the way I would for a paying client, and wrote up what I found.

I send a few of these a month, only to brands where the ads are visibly outrunning the funnel. That's the exact problem I fix, and showing you beats anything I could claim.

## 3. What I actually sell

Most creative help arrives as hands. More variations, more volume, more of whatever already isn't teaching you anything. What I sell is the loop that decides what gets tested and why.

I read your ad account the way I read your funnel in that teardown: winners, losers, and what each one says about the belief your buyer is still missing. Each read becomes a hypothesis. Each hypothesis becomes a brief — net-new ad concepts, a pre-sell page, an advertorial, a rebuilt close on your product page. Production runs through my own Claude Code skill files, which encode the frameworks and pattern libraries from four decades of my campaigns, so you get 2026 throughput with a senior brain deciding what good looks like. Then the results come back and the next round gets smarter.

Your media buyer keeps the spend and the account. I own what gets tested and why. What you end up with is a funnel that compounds: every test carries an explicit hypothesis, every winner generates the next test, every loser teaches you something specific about your buyer.

That is what the teardown was. One turn of the loop, done cold, from the outside.
