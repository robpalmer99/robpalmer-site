<DefinitionBox term="Claude Code Skill">
A folder containing a SKILL.md file: YAML frontmatter with a name and a trigger description, followed by the instructions Claude follows when a task matches. Skills live in a project's .claude/skills/ folder or in ~/.claude/skills/ for every project, and Claude Code loads them automatically or on a slash command such as /copychief. The format is Anthropic's Agent Skills specification, so the same file also works in Claude.ai projects, through the API, and in other agents that support the spec.
</DefinitionBox>

## How this list was built

I ranked on three things. First, what the skill actually tells Claude to do: named formulas, awareness levels, AI tells to avoid, review criteria, compliance rules, or just "be clear and compelling". Second, who wrote it and what they have shipped, because a skill is a distillation of its author's taste and you inherit that taste. Third, maintenance and licence, checked on GitHub on 10 November 2026, with star counts quoted as of that date.

What I didn't rank on is the size of the repository. Several of the entries below are one copywriting skill inside a catalogue of hundreds. The catalogue's stars tell you how many developers found it. They tell you nothing about the one file you'd be installing.

## 1. Rob Palmer: claude-code-copywriting-skills

**Repo:** [github.com/robpalmer99/claude-code-copywriting-skills](https://github.com/robpalmer99/claude-code-copywriting-skills). 49 stars, 10 forks, CC-BY-4.0.

**What's in it:** Five skills that cover a direct response workflow end to end. [direct-response-copy](/blog/claude-code-direct-response-copy-skill) for sales pages, VSLs, emails and headlines. [ad-copy](/blog/claude-code-ad-copy-skill) for Meta, Instagram and UGC scripts, with a Meta specs reference. [landing-page-copy](/blog/claude-code-landing-page-copy-skill) for bridge and pre-sell pages. [copychief](/blog/claude-code-copychief-skill) for structured review against a brief. [compliance-checker](/blog/claude-code-compliance-checker-skill) with a trigger-words reference for Meta, Google, TikTok and the FTC.

**What it makes Claude do:** Write to a Schwartz awareness level, pick a lead type, run the So What chain on every benefit, attach proof to the objection it answers, and avoid a named list of AI tells. The review skill grades a draft the way a copy chief does, with a conversion score and the top three fixes rather than line edits. The compliance skill is the one nobody else on this list has.

**Where it's weakest:** It's opinionated about direct response, so if you want brand copy for a SaaS homepage it will push harder for the sale than you may like. And the skills are the public versions of what I run, without my private vault of 1,239 frameworks and swipes, so they encode the method rather than the library.

**Best for:** Supplement, financial, info-product and DTC offers on paid traffic. Anyone who has to submit ads for review. Written from 40 years of campaigns and [$523 million in tracked results](/blog/523m-copywriting-campaign), which is the thing I can offer that a catalogue can't.

## 2. Corey Haines: marketingskills

**Repo:** [github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills). 59,423 stars, 8,678 forks, MIT.

**What's in it:** 49 marketing skills, of which copywriting, copy-editing, emails, offers, ad-creative, cro and marketing-psychology are the ones a copywriter will use. A product-marketing context file sits underneath them all, so every skill reads your positioning before it writes.

**What it makes Claude do:** The copywriting skill is the best-engineered general page-copy skill I've seen. It diagnoses the input first (blank brief, a draft with a real voice, or a point edit) and limits how much it's allowed to change, which stops the model imposing its own taste on a founder who already made good choices. It bans the "it's not X, it's Y" pattern and the "no X, no Y, no Z" tricolon by name, refuses to invent stats or customers, and names the evidence that would unlock a declined claim.

**Where it's weakest:** It's built for SaaS and startup pages, so it assumes a product-aware visitor and a demo or trial as the action. There's no VSL or long-form sales letter structure, no awareness-level routing, and nothing about compliance. For a cold-traffic health offer it will produce clean, honest, under-powered copy.

**Best for:** Technical founders and SaaS marketers. If your page needs to be clear more than it needs to be persuasive, start here.

## 3. boraoztunc: skills

**Repo:** [github.com/boraoztunc/skills](https://github.com/boraoztunc/skills). 460 stars, 70 forks, Apache-2.0.

**What's in it:** A design-heavy catalogue with a copywriting cluster inside it: copywriting, copy-editing, landing-page, page-cro, pricing-page, ogilvy and stop-slop.

**What it makes Claude do:** The copywriting skill shares its opening with Corey Haines's (same context questions, same page types), so treat it as a sibling. The two that earn the install are copy-editing, which tightens a draft without rewriting its argument, and stop-slop, a sweep for the vocabulary and rhythm that mark a paragraph as machine-written. The ogilvy skill is a nice touch: headline-first, research-led, long copy when the product deserves it.

**Where it's weakest:** Breadth over depth. Most of the catalogue is front-end design, and the copywriting skills are competent generalists rather than specialists.

**Best for:** Designers and developers who write their own landing pages and want the copy to stop reading like AI before a human sees it.

## 4. borghei: Claude-Skills

**Repo:** [github.com/borghei/Claude-Skills](https://github.com/borghei/Claude-Skills). 1,083 stars, 205 forks, custom licence.

**What's in it:** 403 skills across every function. The marketing folder has copywriting, copy-editing, email-sequence, content-humanizer, paid-ads, landing-page-generator and launch-strategy among 40-odd others, plus agent definitions that chain them.

**What it makes Claude do:** The copywriting skill is framework-driven: headline formulas, CTA patterns, objection handling, written for homepages, pricing and feature pages. Content-humanizer is the interesting one, a dedicated pass for AI tells that most teams would otherwise run by hand. The agents (brand-strategist, growth-marketer, marketing-analyst) let you run a sequence without prompting each step.

**Where it's weakest:** With 403 skills, quality varies by folder, and the marketing ones read as thorough rather than opinionated. The licence is custom, so read it before using the skills inside a product.

**Best for:** Marketing teams that want one install covering strategy, copy, email, ads and analytics, and will tune the skills afterwards.

## 5. OneWave AI: claude-skills

**Repo:** [github.com/OneWave-AI/claude-skills](https://github.com/OneWave-AI/claude-skills). 422 stars, 63 forks, MIT.

**What's in it:** Around 230 skills for sales, marketing, design and engineering. The copy-relevant ones are landing-page-copywriter, email-subject-line-optimizer, cold-email-sequence-generator, review-response-writer, brand-voice-analyzer and a compliance-checker.

**What it makes Claude do:** Task-shaped output. Each skill does one job with a defined deliverable, which makes them easy to drop into an automation. The brand-voice-analyzer is useful before any of the writing skills: it reads your existing copy and produces a voice profile the others can follow.

**Where it's weakest:** The writing skills are shallower than the specialist ones above, and the compliance-checker is a general business compliance pass rather than an ad-platform one, so don't rely on it before a Meta submission.

**Best for:** Sales and ops teams building pipelines where copy is one step among many.

## 6. The Boring Marketer: The Direct Response Copy Skill

**Where:** [A public GitHub gist](https://gist.github.com/boringmarketer/96192770df22ac2a9ff4aed72b4c20f4), published 27 January 2026. No licence stated.

**What's in it:** One long skill file, roughly 15,000 words, covering headlines, opening lines, curiosity gaps, the slippery slide, pain quantification, founder stories, testimonials, disqualification and CTAs, with the classic frameworks (Schwartz, Hopkins, Ogilvy, Halbert, Caples, Sugarman, Collier) alongside modern internet-native examples.

**What it makes Claude do:** Write long, specific, story-led copy in the voice of the creator economy: founder narratives, build-in-public proof, Marc Lou and Justin Welsh style examples. It's the closest thing on this list to a complete course in one prompt.

**Where it's weakest:** Length is a cost. A 15,000-word skill eats context on every call, and the internet-native voice is a poor fit for a 55-year-old supplement buyer on native ads. There's no review companion, so the draft it produces is the draft you edit.

**Best for:** Creators and solo founders selling courses, templates and software to an online-native audience.

## 7. cyrwheelninja: copywriting-skill

**Repo:** [github.com/cyrwheelninja/copywriting-skill](https://github.com/cyrwheelninja/copywriting-skill). 11 stars, no licence.

**What's in it:** A single copywriting skill with a resources folder: 50-plus frameworks, an AI trope detector and 34 prompts adapted from Copyhackers.

**What it makes Claude do:** Apply a named framework to a brief and then check its own output for AI tropes. The Copyhackers lineage shows in the conversion-copy vocabulary (voice-of-customer mining, message hierarchy, the Rule of One).

**Where it's weakest:** A single push in February 2026 and no updates since, and no licence, so you can read and learn from it but have no formal right to redistribute. Treat it as a reference rather than a dependency.

**Best for:** Studying how someone else structured a big framework library into one skill before you build your own.

## 8. Anthropic: skills (the official reference)

**Repo:** [github.com/anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills). 190,998 stars.

**What's in it:** No copywriting skill. It's here because it is the specification every entry above follows, with the skill-creator skill, a template and the production document skills.

**What it makes Claude do:** Build you a skill. Run skill-creator on a folder of your own winning copy and your brand rules and it will scaffold a SKILL.md you can then refine. If none of the seven above fits your voice, this is the fastest route to one that does.

**Best for:** Anyone who wants their own skill rather than someone else's taste. Also worth reading before you install anything, so you understand what a skill can and cannot do.

## How to choose

Match the skill to the copy, not the star count.

- **Cold traffic, paid, regulated vertical.** You need awareness-level routing, a review pass and a compliance pass. Install the robpalmer99 suite and run direct-response-copy, then copychief, then compliance-checker, in that order.
- **SaaS or startup pages.** Corey Haines's copywriting and copy-editing skills, with the product-marketing context file filled in first. Add his offers skill if the pricing page is the problem.
- **A draft that reads like AI.** boraoztunc's stop-slop or borghei's content-humanizer, then a human read. Neither fixes a weak argument; both fix the surface.
- **Your own voice, your own winners.** Anthropic's skill-creator with a folder of your best-performing copy as the input. It takes an afternoon and you own the result.

Whichever you pick, feed it references. A skill tells Claude how to write. Your winning controls tell it what winning looks like for your audience, and [six months of running these skills](/blog/claude-code-copywriting-skills) taught me that the second input matters more than the first.

## Installing a skill in 60 seconds

Every entry on this list uses the same layout, so the steps are the same whichever you choose.

1. Create a folder in `.claude/skills/` inside your project, or in `~/.claude/skills/` if you want the skill in every project. Name it after the skill: `copychief/`.
2. Save the skill file inside it as `SKILL.md`. If the skill ships with references (ad specs, trigger words, swipes), they go in a `references/` subfolder inside the same skill folder.
3. Open Claude Code. The skill is detected automatically from the description in its frontmatter, and you can call it directly with a slash command such as `/copychief`.

To install a whole suite, clone the repository and copy the folders you want. The same SKILL.md also works uploaded to a Claude.ai project or attached through the API, so a skill you tune in Claude Code travels with you.

## Where a skill stops and a copywriter starts

A skill fixes the most common failure of AI copy, which is that the model has no taste of its own and defaults to the average of everything it has read. Give it a point of view and the output improves faster than any prompt trick. That's enough for most of the copy most businesses write.

It isn't enough for the page that decides whether an offer scales. Choosing the angle, knowing which claim will survive the platform reviewer, reading why the control is still winning after the challenger looked better on paper: that judgement comes from campaigns, and no skill file has run one. I built mine so the volume could come from the machine and the decisions could stay with me. If you're at the point where the decisions are the bottleneck, the skills are free and [a strategy call](/call) is where the rest of the work starts.
