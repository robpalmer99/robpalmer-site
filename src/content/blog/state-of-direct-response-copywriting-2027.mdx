## Where Does Direct Response Stand Going Into 2027?

It's a bigger industry than it has ever been, and the work inside it has changed more in two years than in the twenty before.

Start with the money. The IAB's annual revenue report, compiled by PwC, found that US digital advertising "drove record revenue, [reaching $294.6 billion in 2025](https://www.iab.com/news/digital-ad-revenue-climbs-to-nearly-300b-as-iab-celebrates-30-year-anniversary/), reflecting a 13.9% year-over-year increase," with social media alone at $117.7 billion. In September 2026 the IAB [raised its forecast](https://www.iab.com/?p=212003) for the year's US ad spend growth to 12.3 percent. The Census Bureau reports that e-commerce [accounted for 17.9 percent](https://www.census.gov/retail/ecommerce.html) of total US retail sales in the third quarter of 2026.

Nearly all of that spending is measured by response: a click, a lead, a sale. That is direct response, whether or not the people buying it use the name.

I've been writing [direct-response copy](/blog/what-is-direct-response-copywriting) for more than 40 years, from direct mail for Apple, IBM and Microsoft through a nine-year campaign for [Belron](/case-studies/belron-safelite-523m-campaign) to VSLs and funnels for ClickBank and supplement offers, with $523M+ in tracked results across the record. This is my assessment of the year. I've written four detailed reports alongside it, on [VSLs](/blog/state-of-vsl-marketing-2027), [AI copywriting](/blog/state-of-ai-copywriting-2027), [email](/blog/state-of-email-marketing-2027) and [landing pages](/blog/state-of-landing-pages-2027). This one is the overview.

## What Changed Since the 2026 Edition?

Five things, and one of them is me.

**Traffic cost more.** [Meta reported](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) its average price per ad up 14 percent year over year in the third quarter of 2026. Every weak page and every tired ad got more expensive to run.

**Volume became the price of entry.** The ad platforms now retrieve from enormous pools of creative and reward advertisers who keep feeding them. The benchmark data below shows how few of those ads win.

**AI became ordinary.** In the UK's [ProCopywriters survey](https://www.procopywriters.co.uk/survey-2026/) of 573 copywriters, "almost three-quarters of copywriters now use generative AI." A year ago that was still a talking point. Now it's the baseline.

**Regulators named names.** Supplement brands, business-opportunity sellers, weight-loss marketers and retailers with fake timers all appeared in enforcement actions.

**AI summaries started taking clicks.** People who see an AI summary in search click through to a site about half as often, [according to Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/), and the effect has reached advertising. On its third-quarter call, the native advertising company Teads reported that the performance side of its business was "down 50%" and blamed "AI-generated search summaries," [as PPC Land reported](https://ppc.land/teads-revenue-drops-26-as-direct-response-profit-halves/).

**I changed my own title.** In October 2026 I repositioned my site from direct-response copywriter to creative strategist and direct-response copywriter. I'll explain why below.

### What I got wrong, and right, last year

I called the central change the Great Bifurcation: routine copy collapsing in price while strategic work held its value, with the middle squeezed. The data since supports it. Researchers at the [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/the-winners-and-losers-of-generative-ai-in-the-freelance-job-market/) found demand for substitutable freelance skills such as writing fell 20 to 50 percent, while the ProCopywriters survey shows "the median freelance day rate is £440, up 10% in two years."

I said AI could not architect a persuasion system and was a force multiplier for experienced writers. I'd still say the first part. On the second I went further than I expected: AI now produces first versions of much of my work, inside a method I've written down.

I said webinars were fading as a conversion tool and that hybrid interactive VSLs were one to watch. Both are still my observation and nothing more. I haven't found data that settles either.

I said single-channel businesses were fragile. Ask anyone who depended on native traffic this year.

## Why Has the Job Become Creative Strategy?

Because the platforms changed what wins, and it stopped being one great ad.

Motion's [Creative Benchmarks 2026](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/methodology) studied 578,750 Meta ad creatives from 6,015 advertiser accounts, launched between September 2025 and January 2026. Its [headline finding](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/winners-are-rare): "Roughly five percent of creatives on Meta spend at least 10× their account median." One ad in twenty carries the account. The same study found that advertisers in its top spending tier test [about 18.8 new creatives a week](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/testing-volume-by-tier).

Put those two numbers together. If one in twenty wins and you launch two a month, you may wait a year for a winner. The accounts that scale are producing ads by the dozen, and the question that decides their results is which dozens.

That question is the job now. Somebody has to read the customer research, find the angles nobody else is running, decide which hooks deserve a test, and make sure twenty different ads still lead to one coherent sales argument. The title the market has given that person is creative strategist. I've set out how it differs from the copywriter's role in [creative strategist vs copywriter](/blog/creative-strategist-vs-copywriter).

It's the reason I changed my own positioning. For most of my career a client hired me for an asset: a letter, a page, a script. In the last two years the clients getting the best results have wanted the map first, meaning the angles, the mechanism and the test plan, and then the assets written from it. The cold-traffic funnel I wrote for Conor Reynolds, which converts at 8 percent at the sales page, was built that way: the video and the page came from the same angle map.

<DefinitionBox term="Creative Strategist">
The person who decides what a brand's advertising should say and test. A creative strategist turns customer research and performance data into angles, hooks, mechanisms and offers, briefs the ads and pages built from them, and reads the results to choose what to make next. In direct response the role sits between the copywriter, who writes the assets, and the media buyer, who places them.
</DefinitionBox>

My service page for this work is [creative strategist](/services/creative-strategist). If what you need is a senior writer for one asset, that's still [direct-response copywriting](/services/direct-response-copywriter), and it's still most of what I do.

## What Is Working Right Now?

The same formats as ever, held to a higher standard.

**The VSL.** Still my first choice for cold traffic on native and YouTube. Three of mine did $30 million, $40 million and $27 million on single offers. The scripts winning now are shorter and denser than they were. The detail is in my [VSL report](/blog/state-of-vsl-marketing-2027).

**The long-form text page.** On Facebook and Instagram traffic it often matches or beats video. The Abundant text sales letter I wrote reached 340 percent ROAS on Facebook cold traffic, and the page I wrote for Stonehenge Health's Dynamic Brain produced $2.1 million in its first 90 days, as reported by the client. Most scaled offers run both formats on one page.

**Email flows.** The automated sequences earn far more per send than broadcasts. Klaviyo's 2026 data has flows producing nearly 41 percent of email revenue from 5.3 percent of sends. The seven-email onboarding sequence I wrote for Chatterkick lifted trial-to-paid conversion by 22 percent. See the [email report](/blog/state-of-email-marketing-2027).

**The pre-sell page.** An advertorial or bridge page between the ad and the offer has become standard in supplements and e-commerce, because it lets a cold reader meet the problem before meeting the product.

**The complete funnel.** Ads, page, video, emails and upsells written from one argument. For Ben Palmer, a ClickBank Platinum vendor, I wrote an email-driven health funnel with every email built on one angle, and it gets 300 percent ROAS.

**Direct mail.** It never left. According to [Winterberry Group](https://winterberrygroup.com/news/winterberry-group-sees-brands-rapidly-adopting-ai-to-support-performance-marketing-efforts-with-overwhelming-majority-seeing-gains), "direct mail spending in the United States is expected to increase by 1.1 percent in 2026 to $36.6 billion," and the US Postal Service's [results for fiscal 2026](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/1110-usps-reports-fiscal-year-2026-results.htm) show "Marketing Mail revenue increased $843 million, or 5.3 percent," on 1.1 percent fewer pieces. Fewer letters, each paying more. A physical mailbox is one of the few places left with no algorithm between you and the customer.

## Which Channels Deserve Your Budget?

Here's my read, with the data I can find for each.

**Meta and YouTube** carry most paid direct-response volume, at rising prices. Both now label AI-made creative.

**Email** is the channel you own, and the one the others exist to feed.

**SMS** has earned a place beside it. Klaviyo's [2026 SMS benchmarks](https://klaviyo.com/products/sms-marketing/benchmarks) put click rates at "5.6% for campaigns and 9.65% for flows," well above email, on far smaller lists.

**TikTok Shop** is now a serious outlet for supplements. Reporting from the industry's main trade show in March 2026, [New Hope Network](https://newhope.com/market-data-and-analysis/supplement-industry-sees-big-growth-big-challenges-in-2026) called it "the emerging channel of the moment" and noted that "vitamins and supplements are the largest category on the platform."

**Affiliates** are larger than most owners assume. The Performance Marketing Association [found](https://thepma.org/pma-study-affiliate-marketing-industry-grows-49-8-to-13-63-b-outpacing-e-commerce-growth-by-2x/) that US affiliate marketing spend "increased by 49.8% from $9.1 billion in 2021 to $13.62 billion in 2024."

**Native advertising** is the one to watch with care. It has been a dependable source of cold traffic for advertorials and VSLs, and it depends on publishers whose own traffic is being reduced by AI summaries.

**Search and AI assistants.** This one is new. At least two of the people who booked calls with me in the last few months found me by asking an AI assistant who to hire. The pages those assistants cited were dated, specific and opinionated. If a buyer in your market asks a machine for a recommendation, it's worth knowing what the machine has read about you.

## What Is AI's Role in Direct Response Now?

It does the drafting and the volume, and a person does the deciding.

I've covered this in full in the [State of AI Copywriting](/blog/state-of-ai-copywriting-2027). The short version: AI is good at research, variations and first versions, it's weak at leads, at cutting and at telling the truth about facts, and the published evidence on whether its copy converts is thin. The advantage comes from the method you wrap around it. I've released [eight of the skills I work with](/blog/claude-code-copywriting-skills) free.

The performance marketing world has adopted it wholesale. In Winterberry Group's panel, "nearly 96 percent of panelists said they have used AI to support direct mail performance," which tells you how far it has spread beyond digital.

## What Did Regulators Do This Year?

They stopped issuing guidance and started bringing cases. I'm a copywriter and this isn't legal advice.

**Supplements.** The US market keeps growing: Nutrition Business Journal [reported](https://smb.theleesvilleleader.com/article/Nutrition-Business-Journal-Releases-2026-Supplement-Business-Report-US-Market-Surges-Toward-dollar100-Billion-Milestone/6a1efc81004597ee6ba1493d) that it "reached $74.15 billion in sales in 2025, posting a robust 7.1% growth rate." Scrutiny grew with it. In April 2026 the FTC charged the supplement brand TruHeight under its reviews and testimonials rule, which I covered in the [VSL report](/blog/state-of-vsl-marketing-2027).

**Weight loss.** In March 2026 the FDA announced "[the issuance of 30 warning letters to telehealth companies](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-warns-30-telehealth-companies-against-illegal-marketing-compounded-glp-1s)" over the marketing of compounded GLP-1 drugs. If your offer sits anywhere near that category, the claims you could make a year ago aren't the claims you can make now.

**Business opportunities.** In March 2026 the FTC [settled with Air AI](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/air-ai-its-owners-will-be-banned-marketing-business-opportunities-settle-ftc-charges-company-misled), an AI-themed business opportunity seller, over allegations that included false earnings claims. The proposed order bans the company and its operators from the industry and "includes a monetary judgment of $18 million, which will be largely suspended." Two months earlier, the Growth Cave defendants were permanently banned from selling business opportunities. The FTC has also [proposed](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-proposes-rule-changes-new-rule-deter-deceptive-earnings-claims-multilevel-marketers-money-making) changes that "would expand the Business Opportunity Rule to cover money-making opportunities," including business coaching. I haven't found a final rule, so treat that as pending.

**Urgency and subscriptions.** UK fines for fake countdown timers, a Washington State ruling that turned misleading email subject lines into a wave of lawsuits, and a federal click-to-cancel rule that was struck down and is being restarted. The [email report](/blog/state-of-email-marketing-2027) has the detail.

Reviews matter more than ever as the thing a buyer trusts. In BrightLocal's [2026 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) of 1,002 US adults, the share who trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations "still sits at a significant 49% today." That trust is exactly what the fake-review cases are about.

My practice hasn't changed. I write the first draft to sell as hard as the product deserves. Then it gets a separate compliance pass, where every risky line is replaced with a legal one doing the same job. Deleting the line and leaving a hole is how compliant copy stops converting.

## What Do Direct Response Copywriters Earn Now?

The top has held, the bottom has collapsed, and the published data covers neither well.

The best independent figure I can find is British. The ProCopywriters survey of 573 writers reports a median freelance day rate of £440 in 2026. That's all kinds of copywriter, not direct response alone.

For senior direct-response work in the US, the ranges I see quoted, and quote myself in my pricing guides, are roughly $15,000 to $35,000 for a long-form sales page and $20,000 to $50,000 for a VSL, with royalties common at the top. Routine content is at the other end: these are my working numbers, but work that cost $300 to $500 a piece in 2023 is now produced for $50 to $100 with AI and an edit.

## What Should a Business Owner Do in 2027?

In this order.

1. **Prove one asset on cold traffic.** A VSL or a sales page that converts strangers. Everything else multiplies it.
2. **Write the flows behind it.** Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back.
3. **Build the angle map.** The list of arguments your ads will run on, drawn from what customers say.
4. **Produce ads in volume from that map,** and expect one in twenty to carry the account.
5. **Run the compliance pass** on every claim, testimonial and deadline.
6. **Write your method down** and put AI to work inside it.
7. **Check what AI assistants say about you.** Buyers are asking them.
