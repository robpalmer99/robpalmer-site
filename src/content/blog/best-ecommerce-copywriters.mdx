Ecommerce copywriting in 2026 is three jobs wearing one title. Someone has to write the ads that stop the scroll on Meta and TikTok. Someone has to write the page the ad sends people to, so the promise in the hook is the first thing the visitor sees. And someone has to write the email and SMS that turn a first order into a customer. The best DTC brands have all three covered, and almost nobody has one person who does all three well.

I'm on it, at number one for ad creative and the pages behind it, and I'll be straight about why: forty years of direct response, $523 million in tracked results, and a [creative strategy](/services/creative-strategist) practice for DTC brands that writes the angle, the ad and the lander as one argument.

## Why ad creative is the bottleneck now

A winning ad on Meta used to run for a quarter. Since the Andromeda update it can fatigue in two or three weeks, because the system matches ads to people by reading the creative itself and rewards accounts that feed it more genuinely different concepts. The constraint has moved from targeting to how many strong angles you can get into the account, and how fast.

That changes who you hire. A copywriter who delivers one polished asset a month can't keep an account fed. An agency that produces volume from a junior writer's brief fills the account with versions of one idea. What scales is a senior brain finding the angles and writing the control, with production behind it. I've written about the [creative strategist](/blog/hire-a-creative-strategist) role in detail, and about whether a [copywriting agency is worth it](/blog/is-a-copywriting-agency-worth-it) for a brand at this stage.

## What ecommerce copywriters charge

Senior DTC copywriters and creative strategists charge $5,000 to $25,000 a month on retainer for ongoing ad creative and the pages behind it, and $5,000 to $15,000 for a landing page or advertorial as a project. A set of ad copy variations from a senior writer runs $3,500 to $7,500. Agencies that bundle production charge $15,000 to $50,000 or more a month, often with a percentage of ad spend at scale. Email agencies price by flow count and list size. For the wider market, see [how much a direct-response copywriter costs](/blog/how-much-does-a-direct-response-copywriter-cost).

## The five questions that expose an ecommerce copywriter

**"Which DTC brands have you written for, and what did the creative do?"** Cost per acquisition, ROAS, hook rate, hold rate. Not impressions.

**"Show me a losing test and what it taught you."** A writer who has never lost a test has never run one with real spend.

**"How do you find angles, and how many net-new concepts do you ship a week?"** You want research from reviews, forums and competitor ads, and a number that isn't one.

**"Can you write the page as well as the ad?"** The ad and the page have to tell one story. Two writers rarely manage it.

**"What would you change about my best ad right now?"** Send it before the call. A specialist comes back with a hypothesis.
